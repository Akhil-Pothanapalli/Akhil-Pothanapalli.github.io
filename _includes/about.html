Meanwhile, in Hyderabad…

Most of what we know is spoken, not written.

Language models learned to read before they learned to listen. The written web got them far, but conversations, consultations, lectures, and phone calls carry meaning that never makes it into text: who is speaking, how they say it, and what happens in the silences between.

That gap is where I work. I care about machines that can reason over what they hear, follow an hour of audio without losing the thread, understand speakers across languages, and hold a conversation the way people actually do: talking and listening at the same time.

Voice is the oldest interface humans have. I think it's about to become the newest one for machines, and I want to help build that.
